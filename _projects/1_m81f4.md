**M81F4** is an X-ray source in M81 that shows recurrent flaring activity in archival Chandra observations. I am investigating its long-term behavior, individual flare profiles, and multiwavelength environment to constrain the physical origin of the events.

## Current focus

- Constructing and interpreting the long-term X-ray light curve
- Comparing flare durations, amplitudes, and recurrence behavior
- Refining the Chandra position and examining available HST imaging
- Testing possible compact-object and accretion scenarios

M81F4 appears in the broader timing survey of X-ray sources in M31, M81, and Centaurus A {% cite zhang2026variability %}, but is treated here as a current research project rather than a separate publication.
