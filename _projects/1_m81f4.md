**M81F4** is an unusual persistent X-ray source in M81 that undergoes recurrent luminous flares in archival Chandra observations. I am characterizing its long-term behavior, individual flare profiles, and multiwavelength environment to determine whether the events arise from an accreting compact binary and what controls their recurrence.

## Current focus

- Constructing and interpreting the long-term X-ray light curve
- Comparing flare durations, amplitudes, and recurrence behavior
- Refining the Chandra position and examining available HST imaging
- Testing possible compact-object and accretion scenarios

M81F4 first emerged from the broader timing survey of X-ray sources in M31, M81, and Centaurus A {% cite zhang2026variability %}. A dedicated paper, **“An Unusual Persistent X-ray Source in M81 Exhibiting Recurrent Luminous Flares,” is in preparation**. No journal, DOI, or arXiv identifier has been assigned.
