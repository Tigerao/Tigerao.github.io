Nearby galaxies provide known-distance laboratories for studying X-ray binary populations outside the Milky Way. I use two decades of archival Chandra observations to characterize short- and long-timescale variability among hundreds of X-ray sources in the bulges of **M31, M81, and Centaurus A**.

The survey searches for flares, dips, periodic signals, and long-term luminosity changes. Its goal is to connect time-domain phenomenology with accretion physics and the underlying compact-binary populations in different galactic environments {% cite zhang2026variability %}.

## Methods

- Uniform Chandra data reduction and source extraction
- Bayesian Blocks searches for flares and dips
- Period searches in photon-counting time series
- Multi-epoch luminosity and rms–flux analyses
