The central region of M31 contains a rich population of X-ray binaries observed repeatedly by Chandra. Using approximately 2 Ms of archival observations spanning 16 years, we performed a systematic search for periodic X-ray sources in the M31 bulge {% cite zhang2024periodic %}.

Seven periodic sources were identified, including four newly discovered systems. Their eclipsing or dipping behavior provides constraints on orbital periods, accretion geometry, and the close-binary population of a nearby galaxy.

This work also motivated follow-up modeling of **M31 UCXB-1**, an ultra-compact X-ray binary candidate with an approximately 465-second modulation {% cite ma2026ucxb %}.
