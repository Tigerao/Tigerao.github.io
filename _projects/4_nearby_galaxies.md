My broader research interest is the relationship between compact-object populations and their host environments. Nearby galaxies make it possible to combine resolved X-ray source studies with multiwavelength information about gas, stellar populations, and galactic structure.

As part of this work, I contributed to a multiwavelength study of the nuclear environment in M31, combining observations and hydrodynamical simulations to revisit the nature of its ring-like gas structure {% cite li2025ring %}.
